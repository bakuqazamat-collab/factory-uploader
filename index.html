Factory Uploader

Factory Uploader is a private, single-user desktop tool. It runs only on the owner's own computer and uploads original animated videos, created by the owner, to YouTube channels that the owner owns and operates, and schedules their publication.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's own channels with a scheduled publish time (YouTube Data API: videos.insert).
  • Sets the custom thumbnail, title, description and tags (thumbnails.set, videos.update).
  • Reads basic information about the owner's own channels and videos (channels.list, videos.list).

What it does not do

  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It does not search, scrape or collect data about other channels or users.
  • It does not sell, share or use any data for advertising.

Sign-in uses Google OAuth. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
